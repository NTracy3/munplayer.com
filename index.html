Position Memory

Every track remembers exactly where you left off. Switch tracks, close the app, reboot your phone — your position is always there.

⊘

Zero Ads

No banner ads, no interstitials, no video ads before playback. Nothing. The app is free and stays free without selling your attention.

⊛

Fully Offline

No account required. No backend. No data sent anywhere. Your files stay on your device. Works without an internet connection.

⊕

Long-Form First

Designed for 1, 2, 6-hour files. Audiobooks, premium podcasts, interviews, lectures — not three-minute pop songs.

⊞

Skip Controls

±15 second skip buttons built into the player. Jump past an intro, rewind a point you missed. Standard for podcasts, missing everywhere else.

Sort your library by percentage listened. See what you've finished, what's in progress, what you haven't started. Simple and useful.
