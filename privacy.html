Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The Short Version

Munplayer collects no data. None. It works entirely on your device with no internet connection, no account, and no backend infrastructure of any kind.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data whatsoever.

Munplayer does not collect:

  • Personal identifiers of any kind
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • Device information or identifiers
  • Location data
  • Audio files or playback history
  • Crash reports or diagnostics

How the App Works

Munplayer reads audio files stored locally on your device. All playback position data is saved exclusively on your device using local storage. No data is ever transmitted to any server.

The app requires no internet connection to function and contains no third-party SDKs, advertising frameworks, or analytics libraries.

Third Parties

Munplayer does not share data with any third parties because no data is collected in the first place. There are no advertising partners, analytics providers, or data brokers involved.

Children's Privacy

Munplayer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Given the nature of the app, significant changes are unlikely.
